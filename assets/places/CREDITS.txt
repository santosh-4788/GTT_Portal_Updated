NOTE - panchgani.jpg and pune.jpg are 1200 x 675 rather than 1600 x 900, the
same size as nashik.jpg. Panchgani's source is only 1024 px wide, so it was
cropped to 16:9 and scaled up slightly rather than shipped smaller than every
other card in the row. The Aga Khan Palace is all fine repeating detail -
hundreds of arches and balusters - and would not come under 215 KB at 1600 x 900
without visible quality loss, so it ships one size down instead.
