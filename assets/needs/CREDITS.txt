PHOTO CREDITS - SERVICE CARD PHOTOGRAPHS
Gupta Tours & Travels

Straight photographs, not composites. Pasting a catalogue vehicle into a
landscape was tried and abandoned: a car lit in a studio cannot be made to sit
in a photographed street, and every one of them read as a cut-out. The cards
that want a vehicle use the vehicle's own photograph; the rest show the place.

Cards with no file here fall back to the `photo` set on the entry in
frontend/src/site/catalog.ts - the destination photographs, credited in
assets/places/CREDITS.txt, and our own fleet shots, which need no credit.

Each card is cropped to 16:10 at 1080 x 675. Where a licence is share-alike
(CC BY-SA), these versions are available under the same licence.
